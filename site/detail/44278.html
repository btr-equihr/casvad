Indoor treatment.- (1) The Chairperson and the whole-time members shall be covered under a Group Mediclaim Policy with an annual cover up to fifteen lakh rupees for self and family subject to the condition that treatment has been taken as per the terms and conditions of the Group Mediclaim Policy.
