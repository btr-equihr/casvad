51. Authority to whom objection may be made

 

An objection under sub-section (1) of section 74 shall lie to Special Commissioner, Additional Commissioner, Joint Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner, Assistant Commissioner, Value Added Tax Officer and Assistant Value Added Tax Officer:

 

PROVIDED that the Commissioner may, by notice published in the official Gazette, fix the jurisdiction of the respective authority on the basis of territory or pecuniary limit or nature or class of objections or on any other basis that may be deemed appropriate by the Commissioner.
