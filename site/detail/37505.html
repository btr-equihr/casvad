Part III

DISCLAIMER

 

160. Order granting leave to disclaim. -

On the hearing of the application referred to in rule 156, the Tribunal may after hearing the Company Liquidator and such parties as may appear in response to the notices issued, and such other persons appearing and interested as the Tribunal may think fit to hear, grant leave to the liquidator and to disclaim on such terms and conditions if any, as to the Tribunal may deem just and the order granting leave to disclaim shall be in Form WIN 84.
