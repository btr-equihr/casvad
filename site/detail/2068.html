CHAPTER XXII

OFFENCES AND PROSECUTIONS

 

1.[“Contravention of order made during search action.”;]

 

275A. Whoever contravenes any order referred to in the second proviso to sub- section (1) or sub-section (3) of section 132 shall be punishable with 2.[“simple imprisonment for a term up to two years and with fine” ]
