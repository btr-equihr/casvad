Part III

SALE BY COMPANY LIQUIDATOR

166. Procedure at sale. -

 

Every sale shall be held by the Company Liquidator, or, if the Tribunal shall so direct, by an agent or an auctioneer approved by the Tribunal, and subject to such terms and conditions, if any, as may be approved by the Tribunal and all sales shall be made by public auction or by inviting sealed tenders or by electronic bidding or in such manners as the Tribunal may direct.
