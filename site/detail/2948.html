District Of India

India is one of the oldest civilizations with a kaleidoscopic variety and rich heritage. Twenty Eight States and Seven Union Territories span the length and breadth of the Country, marked by diversity in culture and geography but bound together by a common chord of Nationalism. There are 612 Districts in India administered by their respective State/UT Government. "Districts of India" is an endeavour of NIC to provide a one-stop source for all the information about Districts of India at one place on the web.
