CHAPTER XXII

OFFENCES AND PROSECUTION

1[“Failure to afford facility for inspection of books of account during search.”; ]

474. If a person, who is required to afford the authorised officer with the necessary facility to inspect the books of account or other documents, under section 247(1)(ii), fails to do so, he shall be punishable with

2[“simple imprisonment for a term up to six months, or with fine, or with both”]
