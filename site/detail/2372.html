PART IX-B

ADVANCE RULINGS

Application for obtaining an advance ruling.

44E. (1) An application for obtaining an advance ruling under sub-section (1) of section 245Q shall be made 4[in quadruplicate,]—

(a) in Form No.34C in respect of a non-resident applicant referred to in sub-clause (i) of clause (a) of section 245N;

(b) in Form 34D in respect of a resident applicant referred to in sub-clause (ii) of clause (a) of section 245N seeking advance ruling in relation to a transaction undertaken or proposed to be undertaken by him with a non-resident; and

(c) in Form No.34DA in respect of a resident applicant referred to in sub-clause (iia) of clause (a) of section 245N falling within any such class or category of person as notified by the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by 1[item (III) of sub-clause (A) of clause (b)] of that section;

(d) in Form No.34E in respect of a resident falling within any such class or category of person as notified by the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by 2[item (IV) of sub-clause (A) of clause

(b)] of section 245N; and

(e) in Form No.34EA, in respect of an applicant referred to in 3[item (V) of sub-clause (A) of clause (b)] of section 245N of the Act, and shall be verified in the manner indicated therein.
