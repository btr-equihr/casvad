9 Net Owned Funds

Every Nidhi shall maintain Net Owned Funds (excluding the proceeds of any preference share capital) of not less than 1[“twenty lakh"] rupees or such higher amount as the Central Government may specify from time to time.

2[“Provided that every Nidhi existing as on the date of commencement of the Nidhi (Amendment) Rules,2022 shall comply with this requirement within a period of eighteen months from the date of such commencement”.]
