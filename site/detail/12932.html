(1) Every person required by sub-section (1) of section 59 to furnish information shall, within one month of the appointed date, furnish the following certificate to the concerned circle incharge of every circle within whose jurisdiction his place of business is located:

(2) If any change takes place in any of the particulars mentioned in the certificate furnished under sub-rule (1), a fresh certificate, incorporating such changes, shall be furnished to the authority specified in sub-rule (1) within seven days of such change.

(3) For the purpose of sub-section (2) of section 59, any clearing, forwarding or booking agent or a person engaged in the business of transporting goods shall maintain a register in Form VR-VIII.
