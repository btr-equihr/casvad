Display of signboard and fine for breach.

10.

(1) Every dealer registered under the Act shall display a signboard at a conspicuous place at his place of business showing his trade name and address of place of business including premises number, floor, room no., etc., if any.

(2) If a dealer uses more than one trade name, all such names should be displayed on the signboard at the respective places of business.

(3) For any breach of the provisions of sub-rule (1), a dealer shall be punishable with a fine of rupees three hundred for each occasion of such breach.

Explanation.

- For the purposes of rule 9 and this rule, the expression "occasion" shall mean a period of seven working days.
