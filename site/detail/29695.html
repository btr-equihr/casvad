(zfa) “Government Entity” means an authority or a board or any other body including a society, trust, corporation,

  1. set up by an Act of Parliament or State Legislature; or
  2. established by any Government, with 90per cent. or more participation by way of equity or control, to carry out a function entrusted by the Central Government, State Government, Union Territory or a local authority.”.
