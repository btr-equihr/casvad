1[22.Meetings of Commission]

 (1) The Commission shall meet at such times and places, and shall observe such rules and procedure in regard to the transaction of business at its meetings as
may be provided by regulations.

(2) The Chairperson, if for any reason, is unable to attend a meeting of the Commission, the senior-most Member present at the meeting, shall preside at the meeting.

(3) All questions which come up before any meeting of the Commission shall be decided by a majority of the Members present and voting, 2 [and in the event of an equality of votes, the Chairperson or in his absence, the Member presiding, shall have a second or/casting vote:]

Provided that the quorum for such meeting shall be three Members.]

Note:

1.  Subs. by the Competition (Amendment) Act, 2007 for “Benches of Commission”

2.Ommitted by by the Competition (Amendment) Act, 2023 dated 11.04.2023.
