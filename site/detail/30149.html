MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS

  NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 7th November, 2017

 

G.S.R. 1371(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 128, sub section (3) of section 129, section 133, section 134 and section 138 read with section 469 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, namely:-

 

1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Accounts) Amendment Rules, 2017.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

 

2.  In the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, in Annexure, for form AOC-4, the following Form shall be substituted, namely:-

AOC-4

 

[F. No. 1/19/2013-CL-V-Part]

AMARDEEP SINGH BHATIA, Jt. Secy.

 

Note :  The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India vide No. G.S.R. 239(E), dated 31.03.2014 and was amended vide notification number G.S.R. 723(E) dated 14.10.2014, G.S.R. 37(E) dated 16.01.2015, G.S.R. 680(E) dated 04.09.2015 and G.S.R. 742(E) dated 27.07.2016.
