Provisional release of goods, documents and things seized pending adjudication.

1[SECTION 110A.

Any goods, documents or things seized under section 110, may, pending the order of the 2[adjudicating  authority], be released to the owner on taking a bond from him in the proper form with such security and conditions as the 2[adjudicating  authority] may require.

Note :-

1. Inserted by Act 29 of 2006, section 26 (w.e.f. 13.07.2006).

2. Substitution by Act 8 of 2011, section 47, for “adjudicating officer” (w.e.f. 08.04.2011).
