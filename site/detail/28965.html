Chapter XV

Anti-Profiteering

131. Cooperation with other agencies or statutory authorities.- 

Where the 1Director General of Anti-profiteering deems fit, he may seek opinion of any other agency or statutory authorities in the discharge of his duties.

 

 

Note

1. Substituted by Notification No. 29/2018  – Central Tax issued on 6th July,2018

in following rule,

Where the Director General of Safeguards deems fit, he may seek opinion of any other agency or statutory authorities in the discharge of his duties.

following words shall be substtituted namely,

Where the Director General of Anti-profiteering deems fit, he may seek opinion of any other agency or statutory authorities in the discharge of his duties.
