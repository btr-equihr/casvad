Part III

DISCLAIMER

 

159. Service of notice. -

Notice of the date fixed for the hearing of the application referred to in rule 156 shall be in Form WIN 83 and shall be served not less than seven days before the date fixed for the hearing, together with a copy of the application and of the affidavit filed in support thereof, and the notice shall require that any affidavit-in-opposition to the application shall be filed in Tribunal and a copy thereof served on the Company Liquidator not later than two days before the date fixed for the hearing.
