Date : Oct 07, 2026

RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

  1. Urban Consumer Confidence Survey (UCCS) – September 2026
  2. Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – September 2026
  3. Rural Consumer Confidence Survey (RCCS) – September 2026
  4. Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Results of the 102nd Round1
  5. Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey on the Manufacturing sector for Q1:2026-27
  6. Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q2:2026-27
  7. Bank Lending Survey for Q2:2026-27
  8. Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q2:2026-27

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
