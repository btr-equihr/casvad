Part II

WINDING UP BY TRIBUNAL

10. Procedure on substitution.-

Where the Tribunal makes an order substituting a contributory as petitioner in a winding up petition, it shall adjourn the hearing of the petition to a date to be fixed by the Bench and direct such amendments of the petition as may be necessary and such contributory shall, within seven days from the making of the order, amend the petition accordingly, and file two legible and clean copies thereof together with an affidavit in duplicate setting out the grounds, on which he supports the petition and the amended petition shall be treated as the petition for the winding up of the company and shall be deemed to have been presented on the date on which the original petition was presented.
