*1235 A. If any person contravenes any of the provisions of this Code or the rules or regulations made thereunder for which no penalty or punishment is provided in this Code, Such person shall be punishable with fine which shall not be less than one lakhs rupees but which may extend to two crores rupees

Note

1. Inserted by the Amendment:- The Insolvency And Bankruptcy Code (Amemdment) Ordinance, 2017.
