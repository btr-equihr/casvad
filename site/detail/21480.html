9. Additional Expenditure 
 
If during the course of the financial year, it becomes necessary to incur expenditure over and above the provision made in the budget, the Board shall immediately submit to the Lt. Governor the details of the proposed expenditure, and specify the manner in which it is proposed to meet the additional expenditure. The Lt. Governor may either approve the proposed expenditure after making such modifications as he considers necessary or reject it. A copy of the order passed by the Lt. Governor on every such proposal to incur additional expenditure shall be communicated to the board and the auditor, if any, appointment by the Lt. Governor.
