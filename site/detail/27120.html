106. 1[Procedure of Authority, Appellate Authority and National Appellate Authority.]

The Authority or the Appellate Authority 2[or the National Appellate Authority] shall, subject to the provisions of this Chapter, have power to regulate its own procedure.

 

Notes:

1. Substituted by The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2019 dated 01.08.2019

For the word:-

Procedure of Authority and Appellate Authority

The following shall be substituted:-

"Procedure of Authority, Appellate Authority and National Appellate Authority"

 

2. Inserted by The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2019 dated 01.08.2019
