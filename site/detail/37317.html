Part II
WINDING UP BY TRIBUNAL

11. Affidavit-in-objection.-

Any affidavit in objection to the petition under sub-section (1) of section 272 shall be filed within thirty days from the date of order, and a copy of the affidavit shall be served on the petitioner or his authorised representative forthwith and copies of the affidavit shall also be given to any contributory appearing in support of the petition who may require the same on payment of five rupees per page within three working days.
