18.

Adjournments of meetings

 

If there is no quorm as laid down in rule 16, the chairman, shall, after waiting for thirteen minutes after' expiration of the appointed hour, adjourn the meeting to such hour on some other future day as he may reasonably fix. A notice of such adjourned meeting shall be sent to every member of the Board and the business which would have been brought before the original meeting, had there been a quorum thereat, shall be brought before the adjourned meeting and may be disposed off at such meeting, whether there be quorum or not.
