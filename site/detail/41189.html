MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS

NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 7th June, 2021

 

G.S.R. 392(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 469 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules  further to amend the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014, namely:-  

  1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Incorporation) Fourth Amendment Rules, 2021.  

   (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

  1. In the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 38A,-  
  1. in the marginal heading, for the words,   “and  Opening of Bank Account”, the words,   “,Opening of Bank  Account and Shops and Establishment Registration”, shall be substituted;
  2.  in the opening portion,  for the letters “AGILE-PRO”, the letters “AGILE-PRO-S” shall be substituted;
  3. for clauses “(c) and (d)”  relating to “Profession Tax Registration and Opening of Bank Account”,  the following clauses shall be substituted, namely:-  

 “(d) Profession Tax Registration with effect from the 23rd February, 2020;

(e) Opening Bank Account with effect from the23rd February, 2020;

 (f) Shops and Establishment Registration.”.

3. in the Annexure to the said rules, for the e-Form No.INC-35, the following form shall be substituted, namely:-

 

    Form No.INC-35

                                       [F. No. 1/13/2013 CL-V, Vol.IV]      

K.V.R. MURTY, Jt. Secy.

 

Note : The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Subsection (i), vide number G.S.R. 250(E), dated the 31st March, 2014 and last amended vide number G.S.R.158 (E),  dated  the  5th March, 2021.
