CHAPTER XXI

PENALTIES

 

Failure to get accounts audited

[446.If any person fails to get his accounts audited for any tax year or years or furnish the audit report as required under section 63, the Assessing Officer may impose a penalty on such person, which shall be the lesser of–

 

(a)0.5%of the total sales, turnover, or gross receipts in business, orthe gross receipts in profession for such tax year or years;or

 

(b)Rs.150000. ]

Note:

1.Substituted by The Finance Bill 2026 dated 01.02.2026 w.e.f. 01.04.2026
