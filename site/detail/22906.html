142A.  Liability under Act to be first charge .

1[Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any Central Act or State Act, any amount of duty, penalty, interest or any other sum payable by an assessee or any other person under this Act,shall, save as otherwise provided in section 529A of the Companies Act, 1956, the Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and the Financial Institutions Act, 1993 and the Securitisation and Reconstruction ofFinancial Assets and the Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002,  be the first charge on the property of the assessee or the person, as the case may be.]

Note;

1. Ins. by Act 8 of 2011, sec. 51 (w.e.f. 8.4.2011).
